## Derived energy per 100 ms decision window (continuous real-time operation: core power × 0.1 s)
6.25 MHz: 70–77 µJ (compute ≈ the window length: marginal for real time); 12.5 MHz: 115 µJ; 25 MHz: 205–213 µJ; 50 MHz: 385–394 µJ.
Per-inference compute energy alone (dynamic power × compute time) is < 10 µJ at every clock.
An earlier benchmark (315 ms / 0.52 mW at 6.25 MHz) measured a 128-neuron single-layer configuration, with the host protocol included in the latency and the idle-dominated core power.
