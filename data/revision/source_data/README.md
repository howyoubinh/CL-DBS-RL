# Source data

Generated by `scripts/write_rev_source_data.py` from the summary files under `data/revision/`. Per-window closed-loop records (biomarker, parameters, disease state per 100 ms decision) for every controller, patient and protocol are the JSON files in `data/revision/final_cycling`, `final_acute`, `final_robustness`, `final_blocks`, `simple_sweep`, `eval_*`.

- `fig3_AB_trace_patient42.csv`: Fig. 3A-B, per 100 ms window (3000 rows)
- `fig3_CD_cycling_per_patient.csv`: Fig. 3C-D, every condition, cycling, 10 patients (340 rows)
- `fig4_A_simple_controllers.csv`: Fig. 4A, simple-controller sweep (5 patients; _sm10 = 1 s smoothed beta) (41 rows)
- `fig4_B_baselines_per_patient.csv`: Fig. 4B and Supplementary Table S4, baselines incl. sweep and SNN teachers, 10 patients (360 rows)
- `fig5_A_students.csv`: Fig. 5A and Supplementary Table S5, students, cycling, 5 patients (29 rows)
- `fig5_C_fp32_vs_xylosim.csv`: Fig. 5C (means; per patient in fig5_AC_per_patient), students in float32 and on XyloSim, cycling, 5 patients (9 rows)
- `fig5_B_xylo_check.csv`: Fig. 5B, XyloSim vs fp32 on recorded windows (4 rows)
- `fig7_ABCD_robustness.csv`: Fig. 7A-D, perturbations, 5 patients (126 rows)
- `fig7_E_blocks.csv`: Fig. 7E, block lengths, 5 patients (40 rows)
- `fig4_A_per_patient.csv`: Fig. 4A, every plotted controller, per patient (5 patients) (235 rows)
- `fig5_AC_per_patient.csv`: Fig. 5A and C, students in float32 and on XyloSim and the teacher, per patient (5 patients) (170 rows)
- `fig7_per_patient.csv`: Fig. 7A-E, every perturbation and controller, per patient (5 patients; none = unperturbed 60 s blocks) (750 rows)
- `results_acute_per_patient.csv`: Results, acute protocol (40 s PD), 10 patients (240 rows)
- `tableS3_teachers_per_patient.csv`: Supplementary Table S3, replicate and omega-0 teachers, 10 patients (90 rows)
- `tableS7_hardened_per_patient.csv`: Supplementary Table S7, hardened teachers, clean cycling, 10 patients (60 rows)
- `fig2_C_population_metrics.csv`: Fig. 2C (and burst fraction in Results), unstimulated, 3 evaluation patients (42 rows)
- `fig2_E_biomarker_1s.csv`: Fig. 2E, GPi alpha-beta power per 1 s window (first second dropped), 3 patients; spike times and membrane traces for Fig. 2A-B, D are in data/revision/activity/*.json (54 rows)
- `fig6_table1_hardware.csv`: Fig. 6 and Table 1, energy per decision window (measured / estimated as stated) (11 rows)
- `fig6_xylo_hdk_runs.csv`: Fig. 6B and Results (core power per clock), every HDK run (8 rows)
